# Research Question

## My Project's Main Research Question

How does uncertainty in Galactic magnetic-field turbulence affect cosmic-ray positron propagation?

## My Secondary Research Question(After researching the turbulence component)

Could uncertainties in cosmic-ray propagation affect the interpretation of the AMS-02 positron excess and indirect dark matter searches?(the positron excess is an observation from the data obtained by AMS-02)

## Why I want to study this realm and this topic

Professor Takaaki Kajita had a lecture at SCIE in January this year. After having the face-to-face conversation with Professor Takaaki Kajita, I learned that there are many uncertanties behind the experimental observations and physics studies. Researchers need to use careful analysis and calculating to consider how these factors may affect the final results. And that's my beginning of studying the realm of cosmic ray. During the process, I noticed the anomalous positron excess observed by AMS-02 detector, and then I though about that what the cosmic ray experiences before reach the detector and whether there is uncertainty in the process of propagation itself. That's the main motivation for me to do this study.

## Scientific Approach of my study

I will use the Monte Carlo simulation method to simulate the propagation of the cosmic ray positrons under different magnatic-field parameters.

I will begin with a simplified magnetic-field model that without the turbulent component, and then bring the factor of the turbulent components. After that, I will analyze the resulting particle trajectories and the propagation behavior.

My final goal is to obtain the information on how uncertainties in cosmic-ray propagation may affect the interpretation of the positron excess as a possible dark matter signal.
